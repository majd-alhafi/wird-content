# Additional hadith works — 2026-10-22

The owner requested these three works conditionally on their suitability for
the existing Sunni/Athari editorial scope. All three are documented as hadith
references by the Saudi General Presidency of Scholarly Research and Ifta's
Sunnah portal:

- Sahih Ibn Khuzaymah: https://sunna.alifta.gov.sa/Book/Details?bookId=11
- Sahih Ibn Hibban: https://sunna.alifta.gov.sa/Book/Details?bookId=10
- Muwatta Malik: https://sunna.alifta.gov.sa/Book/Details?bookId=7

This supports inclusion as classical Sunni hadith references. It is **not**
a blanket contemporary “Salafi” classification of every theological view of
the authors, and does not authenticate every individual report. Discussions
about Ibn Hibban's theological formulations are not resolved by labelling an
entire historical author. No definitive scholarly grade is invented in the app.
The Muwatta includes prophetic reports, companion/successor statements, juristic
opinions and disconnected reports; the app retains these distinctions as present
in the source text. Sahih Ibn Khuzaymah is the extant published material, not
a reconstruction of missing portions of the original work.
