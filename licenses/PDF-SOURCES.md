# Original PDF editions

The 13 Arabic PDF editions and their 72 readable parts are listed in
manifest.json under each collection's `pdf` field. Every part has its byte
length and SHA-256 from the actual downloaded and inspected original.

Source: IslamHouse.com, individual book page and actual PDF URLs recorded in
the manifest. Audited 2026-10-25. Printed author, editor, publisher, edition,
ISBN and copyright pages are retained unchanged in each original PDF.

Use-policy evidence:
https://github.com/IslamHouse-API/multilingual-quran-hadith-islamic-content-database-api-hub/blob/main/README_AR.md

That content-use policy permits downloading, redistribution, offline use and
inclusion in applications subject to preserving the original text and source,
author/editor/publisher/version attribution, and its other conditions. Do not
claim a new exclusive edition or ISBN. The repository's MIT software license
does NOT license these books. PDFs are not represented as public domain or CC.
Printed publisher copyright notices remain intact. No separate permission
letters from every underlying publisher are claimed.

The official Tabari PDF download is a RAR containing the component PDFs. The
readable extracted parts are mirrored here without re-encoding or changing
their pages. The standalone cover is omitted as a reader item. Parts 25/26
are a combined index file, not two additional volumes of commentary.
The other PDF editions use the publisher's direct HTTPS URLs.

JSON editions retain their separately recorded OpenITI/source licenses.
JSON and scanned-PDF page numbers/bookmarks are different edition locations.
